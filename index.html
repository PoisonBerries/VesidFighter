VESID FIGHTER

A 2-player brawler -- couch or online

Player 1

  • A / D Move
  • W Jump
  • S Block (hold)
  • F Attack
  • G Special
  • H Ultimate

Player 2

  • ← / → Move
  • ↑ Jump
  • ↓ Block (hold)
  • L Attack
  • ; Special
  • ' Ultimate

Ultimates charge up as you deal and take damage -- the gold bar under your special gauge.

Play Online

One player creates a room and shares the code, the other joins with it. Online, either key set (WASD+F/G/H or Arrows+L/;/') controls your fighter.
