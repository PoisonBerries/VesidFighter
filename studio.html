Body Part Studio

Every fighter is built from ten parts. Draw any of them over its template and the game uses your drawing in place of the built-in shape, following all the existing animation. Parts you haven't drawn yet stay as they are, so you can go one limb at a time.

How to draw a part

  1. Download its template and open it in any art app (Procreate, Krita, Photoshop...). Keep the image size.
  2. The faint figure is today's built-in part, as a guide. The two pink crosses are the joints the part hangs between (the hint under each part says which). Keep your drawing's joints on those crosses; the shape around them is yours: bigger arms, different clothes, anything.
  3. Draw on a new layer over the template, then hide the template layer and export a PNG with a transparent background.
  4. Everything faces right (the game mirrors it). Limbs hang straight down; fists and shoes point right. One drawing is used for both the front and the back arm/leg (the back one is shaded automatically).
  5. Check it here with Try my drawing: it shows up on the moving fighter straight away (nothing is saved).
  6. To put it in the game: save it as assets/parts/<character>/<part>.png (the exact file name is shown on each part), then run python3 tools/update-parts.py and commit.
